Connect your wallet to Nib

Press Change wallet on Nib. Its screen shows a QR (this page opens with the code filled in).

Your wallet address (the one that will pay):

Just paste the public address (long-press your wallet's address to copy it). Nib never gets your keys. When you approve a payment, you scan a QR on Nib with your wallet's scanner and confirm in the wallet.
Inside a wallet app's browser you can also connect it instead.
